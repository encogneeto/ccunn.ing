What to say; what to say...

I stopped using Facebook years ago, and never really replaced it with anything as a method of keeping in touch other than awkward and infrequent text messages.

This is partially an attempt to fill that gap, though I regognize its success is fraught with hurdles. Not the least of which is me actually posting here; but on that front I'm giving myself permission to post insignificant thoughts - even those only barely or partially formulated so as to keep the posts coming. Appologies if you've stumbled on low quality posts. Further appologies if you've found a site with NO recent posts.

I'm hopeful that if I am able keep up my end of the bargain, people may be interested enough to come se what I have to say.

That is the hopeful raison d'être

Beyond that this is my little dent in the effort to encourage the old, weird, hopeful web of yore. My modern Angelfire or GeoCity...
